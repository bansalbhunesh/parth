# Owner Design Basis — External Lightning Protection System (Project Basalt)

*Dev-corpus fixture (team/AI-authored). Owner design intent, not a vendor document.*

## 1. Protection class
- The external lightning protection system (LPS) shall be designed to **IEC 62305 Class I**.
- Design lightning current for Type 1 arresters and down-conductor sizing: **100 kA** (10/350 µs waveform).

## 2. Air termination and down conductors
- Air-termination mesh: maximum **10 m × 10 m** grid on all roof areas.
- Down conductors at maximum **10 m** spacing around the building perimeter.

## 3. Earthing interface
- Each down-conductor test point shall achieve an earth resistance of **10 ohms or less**, measured seasonally dry.
- Test joints shall be accessible without dismantling cladding.
