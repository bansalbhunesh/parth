# Vendor Submittal — External Lightning Protection System

*Dev-corpus fixture (team/AI-authored vendor-style datasheet).*

## Scope of supply
Complete external LPS for the data hall and energy centre roofs, engineered to
IEC 62305 **Class I**.

## Air termination
- Roof mesh conductor: 8 mm aluminium, laid at **10 m × 10 m** grid with rooftop
  air rods at plant screens.

## Down conductors
- 25 mm × 3 mm aluminium tape at **10 m** maximum spacing, clipped per
  manufacturer instructions.
- Type 1 surge arresters rated **Iimp 100 kA (10/350 µs)** at the main LV intake.

## Accessories
- Bi-metallic connectors at all aluminium-to-copper transitions.
- Test clamps supplied at each down conductor, mounted 1.5 m above finished
  ground level for access.
