# Owner Design Basis — Diesel Fuel Polishing System (Project Basalt)

*Dev-corpus fixture (team/AI-authored). Owner design intent, not a vendor document.*

## 1. Duty
- Each bulk diesel storage tank shall be served by an automatic fuel polishing
  system turning over the **full tank volume every 24 hours**.

## 2. Fuel quality
- Polished fuel cleanliness shall achieve **ISO 4406 code 18/16/13** or better,
  verified by particle counting at the polisher outlet.
- Water content after polishing: **200 ppm** maximum.

## 3. Controls
- Automatic scheduling with BMS status and common-alarm contacts.
