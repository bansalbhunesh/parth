# Owner Design Basis — Water Leak Detection (Project Basalt)

*Dev-corpus fixture (team/AI-authored). Owner design intent, not a vendor document.*

## 1. Coverage
- Sensing cable shall cover all raised-floor CRAH galleries, pipe routes, and
  drip trays in data halls.

## 2. Response
- A leak event shall be reported to the BMS within **30 seconds** of cable
  wetting, with zone identification to the nearest **1 m**.

## 3. Supervision
- Cable and module circuits shall be supervised; cable break or module loss
  raises a distinct trouble alarm.
