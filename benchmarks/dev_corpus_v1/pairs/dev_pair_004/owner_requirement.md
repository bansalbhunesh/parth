# Owner Design Basis — Battery Room Hydrogen Detection (Project Basalt)

*Dev-corpus fixture (team/AI-authored). Owner design intent, not a vendor document.*

## 1. Detection
- Fixed hydrogen sensors in every VRLA battery room, mounted at the highest
  points of the ceiling void.
- Pre-alarm at **1 percent** hydrogen by volume (25 percent of LEL) reported to
  the BMS and local beacon.

## 2. Interlocks
- Emergency exhaust ventilation shall be hard-wire interlocked to start at
  **2 percent** hydrogen by volume, independent of the BMS.

## 3. Maintenance
- Sensors calibratable in place; calibration interval 12 months or better.
