# Owner Design Basis — Resistive Load Bank (Project Basalt)

*Dev-corpus fixture (team/AI-authored). Owner design intent, not a vendor document.*

## 1. Rating
- Permanently installed resistive load bank rated **2500 kW** continuous at
  **40 degrees C** ambient.

## 2. Control
- Remote load-step control in **25 / 50 / 75 / 100 percent** steps from the
  commissioning control room, with local override.

## 3. Protection
- Over-temperature and airflow-failure trips wired to shunt-trip the supply
  breaker.
