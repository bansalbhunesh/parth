# Owner Design Basis — Earthing Grid (Project Basalt)

*Dev-corpus fixture (team/AI-authored). Owner design intent, not a vendor document.*

## 1. Grid resistance
- Overall combined earth grid resistance shall be **1 ohm** or less, measured
  by fall-of-potential after backfill consolidation.

## 2. Conductors
- Buried grid conductors: bare copper, minimum cross-section **70 mm2**.

## 3. Joints
- Below-grade joints exothermically welded; mechanical clamps are not accepted
  below grade.
