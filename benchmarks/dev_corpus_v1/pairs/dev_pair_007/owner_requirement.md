# Owner Design Basis — Condenser Water Treatment (Project Basalt)

*Dev-corpus fixture (team/AI-authored). Owner design intent, not a vendor document.*

## 1. Chemistry control
- Automatic conductivity-controlled blowdown holding condenser water between
  **1200 and 1800 microsiemens per cm**.
- Alternating oxidising / non-oxidising biocide dosing on a 7-day program.

## 2. Monitoring
- Corrosion coupon rack with mild steel and copper coupons, 90-day exposure
  reporting.
