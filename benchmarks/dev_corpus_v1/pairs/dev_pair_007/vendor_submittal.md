## Description
Skid-mounted dosing and blowdown plant for the condenser water circuit with
duty/standby dosing pumps and automatic controller.

## Dosing program
- Week program alternating oxidising and non-oxidising biocide, 7-day cycle,
  with lockout interlocks during coupon retrieval.

## Monitoring

  laboratory reporting.
