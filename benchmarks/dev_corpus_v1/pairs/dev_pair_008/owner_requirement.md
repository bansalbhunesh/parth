# Owner Design Basis — Emergency Power Off (EPO) System (Project Basalt)

*Dev-corpus fixture (team/AI-authored). Owner design intent, not a vendor document.*

## 7. EPO provisions (§7.2)
- EPO stations at every data hall exit shall be **dual-action** (lift cover,
  then push) to prevent accidental operation.
- EPO wiring shall be **24 V DC supervised** circuits; open or short raises a
  supervisory alarm at the fire command centre.
- Activation logs with station identity retained for 5 years.
