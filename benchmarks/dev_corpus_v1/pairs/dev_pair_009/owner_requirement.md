# Owner Design Basis — Generator SCR and DEF System (Project Basalt)

*Dev-corpus fixture (team/AI-authored). Owner design intent, not a vendor document.*

## 1. Emissions performance
- The selective catalytic reduction system shall limit engine-out nitrogen oxides to **0.67 g/bhp-hr or less** at the declared standby rating.
- Ammonia slip shall not exceed 5 ppm after stabilisation.

## 2. Reductant autonomy
- Usable diesel exhaust fluid storage shall support at least **72 hours** of operation at declared full-load consumption.
- Provide low-level and critically-low-level contacts to the generator control system.
