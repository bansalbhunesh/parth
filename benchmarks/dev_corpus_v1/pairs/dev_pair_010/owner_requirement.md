# Owner Design Basis — Generator Acoustic Attenuation (Project Basalt)

*Dev-corpus fixture (team/AI-authored). Owner design intent, not a vendor document.*

## 1. Environmental noise
- Complete packaged generator sound pressure shall not exceed **65 dBA at 1 m** under full standby load in free-field conditions.

## 2. Attenuators
- Discharge attenuator dynamic insertion loss shall be at least **25 dB** in the 250 Hz octave band.
- Acoustic louvres shall be weather resistant and drain to the exterior.
