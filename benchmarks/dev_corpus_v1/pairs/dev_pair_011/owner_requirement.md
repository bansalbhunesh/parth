# Owner Design Basis — Fire-Rated Cable Transits (Project Basalt)

*Dev-corpus fixture (team/AI-authored). Owner design intent, not a vendor document.*

## 1. Fire integrity
- Modular cable transit assemblies through two-hour barriers shall provide **120 minutes fire integrity** for the installed cable mix.

## 2. Leakage control
- Tested air leakage through each sealed frame shall not exceed **0.25 m3/h at 50 Pa**.
- Every frame shall carry a permanent identification tag linked to its installation record.
