# Owner Design Basis — Generator SCR Performance (Project Cinder)

*Dev-corpus fixture (team/AI-authored). Owner design intent, not a vendor document.*

## 1. Guaranteed emissions
- Nitrogen oxides after the SCR shall not exceed **0.67 g/bhp-hr** at rated standby load.
- Stabilised ammonia slip shall not exceed **5 ppm**.

## 2. DEF storage
- Usable DEF storage autonomy shall be at least **72 hours** at rated-load consumption.
