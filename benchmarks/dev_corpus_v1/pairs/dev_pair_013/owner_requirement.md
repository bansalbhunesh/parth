# Owner Design Basis — Generator Acoustic System (Project Cinder)

*Dev-corpus fixture (team/AI-authored). Owner design intent, not a vendor document.*

## 1. Noise limit
- Full-load free-field sound pressure shall not exceed **65 dBA at 1 m**.

## 2. Air path
- Discharge attenuator insertion loss shall be at least **25 dB at 250 Hz**.
- Acoustic louvre clean pressure drop shall not exceed **100 Pa** at design airflow.
