# Owner Design Basis — Fire-Rated Cable Transit System (Project Cinder)

*Dev-corpus fixture (team/AI-authored). Owner design intent, not a vendor document.*

## 1. Fire and water integrity
- Installed transit assemblies shall provide at least **120 minutes fire integrity**.
- Sealed frames below the flood line shall withstand at least **0.30 bar water pressure** without leakage.

## 2. Future capacity
- Each installed frame shall retain at least **25 percent spare module capacity** after initial cable installation.
