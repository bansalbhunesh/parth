# Owner Design Basis Requirement — Cooling tower

*Team-authored owner-requirement fixture (design basis). Not a vendor document. Requirement values are the owner's design intent.*

## 1. Capacity
- Heat rejection capacity shall be at least **5000 kW**.
- Approach temperature shall be **<= 3 C**.
- Drift shall be **<= 0.001%**.
- Fans shall be **EC variable-speed**.
- Makeup water rate shall be stated.
