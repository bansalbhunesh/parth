# Owner Design Basis Requirement — Standby generator (start)

*Team-authored owner-requirement fixture (design basis). Not a vendor document. Requirement values are the owner's design intent.*

## 1. Start & rating
- Start/accept-load time shall be **<= 10 s**.
- Fuel shall be **diesel**.
- Rated power shall be **2000 kW**.
- Rated voltage shall be **415 V**.
