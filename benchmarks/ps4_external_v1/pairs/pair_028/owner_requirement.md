# Owner Design Basis Requirement — Structured cabling (data hall)

*Team-authored owner-requirement fixture (design basis). Not a vendor document. Requirement values are the owner's design intent.*

## 1. Fire & performance
- Plenum pathways shall use **CMP** cable.
- Copper shall be **Cat6A**.
- Bend radius shall be **>= 4x diameter**.
- Jacket shall be **LSZH**.
- A fire-test listing (UL 910) shall be provided.
