# Owner Design Basis Requirement — Refrigerant (DX unit)

*Team-authored owner-requirement fixture (design basis). Not a vendor document. Requirement values are the owner's design intent.*

## 1. Sustainability
- Refrigerant GWP shall be **<= 750**.
- A refrigerant leak-detection system shall be provided.
- Charge shall be **<= 50 kg** per circuit.
