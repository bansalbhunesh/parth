# Project Meghdoot — Design Basis: Power & Data Cabling
_System: CABLE · Uptime Tier IV · 40 MW_
_Client: Meghdoot Digital Infrastructure Pvt. Ltd._
_EPC: Patel-Larsen JV_

## Overview

Data cabling within IT spaces classified as plenum-rated areas shall carry a CMP (Communications Multipurpose Plenum) fire rating per NFPA 75 room classification requirements. CMR (riser-rated) cable is NOT acceptable in plenum spaces.

## Requirements

- **CABLE-DC** — fire rating: shall be **CMP plenum-class** (ref: NFPA-75; clause DB-8.4)
- **CABLE-DC** — category: shall be **Cat6A type** (ref: TIA-942; clause DB-8.1)
- **CABLE-DC** — max bundle size: shall be **48 cables** (ref: BICSI-002; clause DB-8.5)
