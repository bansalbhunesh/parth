# Project Meghdoot — Design Basis: Cooling — Chillers / CRAH / Liquid Loop
_System: COOL · Uptime Tier IV · 40 MW_
_Client: Meghdoot Digital Infrastructure Pvt. Ltd._
_EPC: Patel-Larsen JV_

## Overview

The chilled-water cooling plant shall maintain ASHRAE-recommended supply conditions across all white-space zones. Redundancy shall satisfy Tier IV fault tolerance: the system must tolerate one chiller failure while another chiller is in planned maintenance, requiring N+2 minimum.

## Requirements

- **COOL-LOOP** — redundancy: shall be **N+2 topology** (ref: UPTIME-TIER4; clause DB-6.1)
- **CRAH** — supply air temp c: shall be **24 C** (ref: DESIGN-BASIS; clause DB-6.3)
- **CHILLER** — capacity tr: shall be **1500 TR** (ref: DESIGN-BASIS; clause DB-6.2)
- **COOL-LOOP** — delta t c: shall be **10 C** (ref: DESIGN-BASIS; clause DB-6.4)
