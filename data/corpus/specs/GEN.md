# Project Meghdoot — Design Basis: Diesel Generators & Fuel Storage
_System: GEN · Uptime Tier IV · 40 MW_
_Client: Meghdoot Digital Infrastructure Pvt. Ltd._
_EPC: Patel-Larsen JV_

## Overview

Standby diesel generators shall support the entire critical and mechanical load for a sustained utility outage. On-site bulk fuel storage shall provide a minimum of 24 hours at full rated load per the Tier IV design basis, ensuring operational continuity without dependence on fuel delivery during the initial outage period.

## Requirements

- **GEN-FUEL** — onsite fuel hours: shall be **24 h** (ref: UPTIME-TIER4; clause DB-5.4)
- **GEN-01** — redundancy: shall be **N+1 topology** (ref: UPTIME-TIER4; clause DB-5.1)
- **GEN-01** — rated power kva: shall be **2500 kVA** (ref: DESIGN-BASIS; clause DB-5.2)
- **GEN-01** — start time sec: shall be **10 s** (ref: DESIGN-BASIS; clause DB-5.3)
