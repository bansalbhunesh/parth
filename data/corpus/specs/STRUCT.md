# Project Meghdoot — Design Basis: Structural — Raised Floor & Seismic
_System: STRUCT · Uptime Tier IV · 40 MW_
_Client: Meghdoot Digital Infrastructure Pvt. Ltd._
_EPC: Patel-Larsen JV_

## Overview

The raised-floor system in all data halls shall be designed for the concentrated and distributed loads of fully populated server racks, including future high-density deployments up to 30 kW/rack. Floor height shall provide adequate clearance for under-floor chilled-air distribution, power cabling, and fire suppression piping, with seismic bracing per IS 1893 for Zone IV.

## Requirements

- **FLOOR** — load rating kpa: shall be **12 kPa** (ref: DESIGN-BASIS; clause DB-13.1)
- **FLOOR** — seismic zone: shall be **Zone_IV class** (ref: IS-1893; clause DB-13.2)
- **FLOOR** — height mm: shall be **900 mm** (ref: DESIGN-BASIS; clause DB-13.3)
