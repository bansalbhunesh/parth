# Project Meghdoot — Design Basis: MV/LV Switchgear
_System: SWGR · Uptime Tier IV · 40 MW_
_Client: Meghdoot Digital Infrastructure Pvt. Ltd._
_EPC: Patel-Larsen JV_

## Overview

MV switchgear shall be rated for the full prospective fault level as determined by the project fault study. The minimum short-circuit withstand rating shall be 50 kA for 1 second, coordinated with upstream utility protection.

## Requirements

- **SWGR-MV** — short circuit rating ka: shall be **50 kA** (ref: DESIGN-BASIS; clause DB-7.2)
- **SWGR-MV** — redundancy: shall be **2N topology** (ref: UPTIME-TIER4; clause DB-7.1)
- **SWGR-MV** — arc flash rating: shall be **Type_2B class** (ref: DESIGN-BASIS; clause DB-7.3)
