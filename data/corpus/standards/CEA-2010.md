# Central Electricity Authority (Measures relating to Safety and Electric Supply) Regulations, 2010

The Central Electricity Authority (CEA) of India sets safety requirements for the installation, operation, and maintenance of electrical supply lines and apparatus.

## 1. Safety Clearances (Regulation 44)

For low, medium, and high voltage lines, safety clearances must be maintained to prevent accidental contact.

### 1.1 Clearance above ground and from buildings
- **Vertical clearance** for open live electrical conductors above ground or accessible surfaces:
  - Low and Medium voltage: **2.5 metres** minimum.
  - High voltage: **3.7 metres** minimum.
- **Horizontal clearance** to any building or structure:
  - Low and Medium voltage: **1.2 metres** minimum.
  - High voltage: **2.0 metres** minimum.

## 2. Earthing and Grounding (Regulation 41)

All metal parts of electrical apparatus, including switchgear enclosures, transformers, and cable trays, must be connected to earth per IS 3043.
- Dual independent earth paths are mandatory for all medium and high voltage installations.
