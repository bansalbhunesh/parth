# Vendor Submittal — Structural — Raised Floor & Seismic (STRUCT)
_Project: Project Meghdoot · Submittal rev B_

## Vendor Notes

Vendor: Kingspan Alpha III HPL system, steel pedestal + stringer.
Panels: 600 × 600 mm, calcium sulphate core, HPL surface.
The vendor has proposed a 600 mm finished floor height based on their standard pedestal range. The vendor notes that 600 mm is the minimum recommended height per BICSI-002 and is adequate for conventional under-floor air distribution. The project specification of 900 mm has been noted but the vendor recommends the lower height to reduce cost and pedestal count.

## Provided values

- **FLOOR** — load rating kpa: **8 kPa** (vendor datasheet)
- **FLOOR** — seismic zone: **Zone_IV class** (vendor datasheet)
- **FLOOR** — height mm: **600 mm** (vendor datasheet)
