# Design Basis: Data Centre Power Systems
## Project: Demo Facility — 10 MW, Uptime Tier III

### UPS System Requirements
- **UPS-01** — battery runtime min: shall be **15 min** (ref: UPTIME-TIER3; clause DB-2.1)
- **UPS-01** — redundancy: shall be **N+1 topology** (ref: UPTIME-TIER3; clause DB-2.2)
- **UPS-01** — efficiency pct: shall be **97 %** (ref: DESIGN-BASIS; clause DB-2.3)
- **UPS-01** — rated power kw: shall be **800 kW** (ref: DESIGN-BASIS; clause DB-2.4)

### Generator Requirements
- **GEN-01** — start time sec: shall be **10 s** (ref: UPTIME-TIER3; clause DB-3.1)
- **GEN-01** — onsite fuel hours: shall be **48 h** (ref: DESIGN-BASIS; clause DB-3.2)
- **GEN-01** — rated power kw: shall be **2000 kW** (ref: DESIGN-BASIS; clause DB-3.3)

### Cooling Requirements
- **COOL-01** — redundancy: shall be **N+1 topology** (ref: UPTIME-TIER3; clause DB-4.1)
- **COOL-01** — delta t c: shall be **12 C** (ref: ASHRAE-TC9.9; clause DB-4.2)

### Cabling Requirements
- **CABLE-01** — fire rating: shall be **CMP plenum-class** (ref: NFPA-75; clause DB-5.1)
- **CABLE-01** — max bundle size: shall be **48 cables** (ref: BICSI-002; clause DB-5.2)
