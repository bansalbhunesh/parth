# Project Athena — Design Basis: Cabling Infrastructure
_System: CABLE · EN 50600 Class 4 · 15 MW_

## Overview

Data cables in plenum and riser spaces shall achieve Euroclass B2ca-s1a,d1,a1 per EN 13501-6 for reduced fire load.

## Requirements

- **CABLE-01** — fire rating: shall be **B2ca euroclass** (ref: EN-13501-6; clause AT-8.1)
- **CABLE-01** — category: shall be **Cat6A type** (ref: EN-50173; clause AT-8.2)
