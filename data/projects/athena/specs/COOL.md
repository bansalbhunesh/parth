# Project Athena — Design Basis: Cooling Plant
_System: COOL · EN 50600 Class 4 · 15 MW_

## Overview

Cooling systems shall use refrigerants with GWP <= 1 per EU F-Gas Regulation phase-down schedule for 2027 compliance.

## Requirements

- **COOL-01** — redundancy: shall be **N+2 topology** (ref: EN-50600; clause AT-6.1)
- **COOL-01** — refrigerant gwp: shall be **1 max** (ref: EU-F-GAS; clause AT-6.2)
- **COOL-01** — capacity tr: shall be **600 TR** (ref: DESIGN-BASIS; clause AT-6.3)
