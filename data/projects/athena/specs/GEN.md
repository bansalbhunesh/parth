# Project Athena — Design Basis: Diesel Generators
_System: GEN · EN 50600 Class 4 · 15 MW_

## Overview

Generators shall comply with EU Stage V emission limits per Regulation 2016/1628 for non-road mobile machinery.

## Requirements

- **GEN-01** — redundancy: shall be **N+1 topology** (ref: EN-50600; clause AT-5.1)
- **GEN-01** — rated power kva: shall be **2000 kVA** (ref: DESIGN-BASIS; clause AT-5.2)
- **GEN-01** — emission class: shall be **Stage_V class** (ref: EU-2016/1628; clause AT-5.3)
