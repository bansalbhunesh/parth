# Project Athena — Design Basis: MV Switchgear
_System: SWGR · EN 50600 Class 4 · 15 MW_

## Overview

MV switchgear shall be SF6-free per EU F-Gas phase-down. Alternatives: dry air, solid insulation, or vacuum.

## Requirements

- **SWGR-01** — short circuit rating ka: shall be **50 kA** (ref: DESIGN-BASIS; clause AT-7.1)
- **SWGR-01** — insulation: shall be **SF6_free type** (ref: EU-F-GAS; clause AT-7.2)
