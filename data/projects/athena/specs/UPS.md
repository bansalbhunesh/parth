# Project Athena — Design Basis: Uninterruptible Power Supply
_System: UPS · EN 50600 Class 4 · 15 MW_

## Requirements

- **UPS-01** — battery runtime min: shall be **10 min** (ref: EN-50600; clause AT-4.1)
- **UPS-01** — redundancy: shall be **2N topology** (ref: EN-50600; clause AT-4.2)
- **UPS-01** — rated power kw: shall be **900 kW** (ref: DESIGN-BASIS; clause AT-4.3)
