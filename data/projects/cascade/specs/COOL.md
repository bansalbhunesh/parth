# Project Cascade — Design Basis: Cooling — Evaporative
_System: COOL · Uptime Tier IV · 30 MW_

## Overview

Water Usage Effectiveness (WUE) target 0.4 L/kWh to meet Oregon water conservation requirements.

## Requirements

- **COOL-C1** — redundancy: shall be **N+2 topology** (ref: UPTIME-TIER4; clause CS-6.1)
- **COOL-C1** — wue target: shall be **0.4 L/kWh** (ref: DESIGN-BASIS; clause CS-6.2)
- **COOL-C1** — capacity tr: shall be **1200 TR** (ref: DESIGN-BASIS; clause CS-6.3)
