# Project Cascade — Design Basis: Fire Suppression
_System: FIRE · Uptime Tier IV · 30 MW_

## Overview

Emergency Power Off (EPO) system required per NFPA 75 and local AHJ. All data hall zones shall have clean-agent suppression.

## Requirements

- **FIRE-C1** — agent: shall be **clean_agent type** (ref: NFPA-75; clause CS-9.1)
- **FIRE-C1** — epo system: shall be **present status** (ref: NFPA-75; clause CS-9.2)
- **FIRE-C1** — zones: shall be **6 count** (ref: DESIGN-BASIS; clause CS-9.3)
