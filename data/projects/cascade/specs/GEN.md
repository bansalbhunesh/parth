# Project Cascade — Design Basis: Standby Generators
_System: GEN · Uptime Tier IV · 30 MW_

## Overview

Generators shall meet EPA 40 CFR Part 60 Tier 4 emission standards for stationary engines.

## Requirements

- **GEN-C1** — redundancy: shall be **N+1 topology** (ref: UPTIME-TIER4; clause CS-5.1)
- **GEN-C1** — rated power kva: shall be **2500 kVA** (ref: DESIGN-BASIS; clause CS-5.2)
- **GEN-C1** — epa tier: shall be **Tier_4 class** (ref: EPA-40CFR60; clause CS-5.3)
- **GEN-FUEL** — onsite fuel hours: shall be **48 h** (ref: DESIGN-BASIS; clause CS-5.4)
