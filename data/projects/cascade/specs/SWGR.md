# Project Cascade — Design Basis: MV Switchgear
_System: SWGR · Uptime Tier IV · 30 MW_

## Overview

Switchgear shall have ICC-ES evaluation report for seismic certification per IBC 2021 for Seismic Design Category D.

## Requirements

- **SWGR-C1** — short circuit rating ka: shall be **50 kA** (ref: DESIGN-BASIS; clause CS-7.1)
- **SWGR-C1** — redundancy: shall be **2N topology** (ref: UPTIME-TIER4; clause CS-7.2)
- **SWGR-C1** — seismic cert: shall be **ICC_ESR standard** (ref: IBC-2021; clause CS-7.3)
