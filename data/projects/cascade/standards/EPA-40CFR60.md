# EPA 40 CFR Part 60 — Stationary Engine Emission Standards (paraphrased)

Emission tiers for stationary compression-ignition engines:
- Tier 1 (pre-2006): basic limits
- Tier 2 (2007-2014): reduced PM and NOx
- Tier 3 (interim): further reductions
- Tier 4 (2015+): near-zero emissions, requires DPF + SCR

Emergency standby engines may qualify for Tier 2 exemption only if
operation limited to <100 hours/year in non-emergency use.
Data centres with >500 hours/year runtime require Tier 4.
