# International Building Code 2021 — Seismic Design (paraphrased)

Seismic Design Categories (SDC) A through F based on site class and location.
- SDC D: moderate-to-high seismic risk (Portland/Hillsboro, Oregon)
- Equipment anchorage: ICC-ES evaluation required
- Raised floor: special seismic bracing per ASCE 7-22
- Essential facilities: Importance Factor Ie = 1.5
