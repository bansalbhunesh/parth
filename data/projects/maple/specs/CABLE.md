# Project Maple — Design Basis: Cabling Infrastructure
_System: CABLE · Uptime Tier III · 12 MW_

## Overview

Data cables in plenum spaces shall be FT6 rated per Canadian Electrical Code 2024 for plenum use.

## Requirements

- **CABLE-01** — fire rating: shall be **FT6 CEC-class** (ref: CEC-2024; clause MP-8.1)
- **CABLE-01** — category: shall be **Cat6A type** (ref: CSA-T529; clause MP-8.2)
