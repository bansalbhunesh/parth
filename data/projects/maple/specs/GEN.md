# Project Maple — Design Basis: Diesel Generators
_System: GEN · Uptime Tier III · 12 MW_

## Overview

Generators shall achieve reliable cold start at -40 C per CSA C282 for Canadian winter extremes.

## Requirements

- **GEN-01** — redundancy: shall be **N+1 topology** (ref: UPTIME-TIER3; clause MP-5.1)
- **GEN-01** — rated power kva: shall be **1800 kVA** (ref: DESIGN-BASIS; clause MP-5.2)
- **GEN-01** — cold start temp c: shall be **-40 C** (ref: CSA-C282; clause MP-5.3)
