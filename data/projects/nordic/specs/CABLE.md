# Project Nordic Edge — Design Basis: Cabling Infrastructure
_System: CABLE · EN 50600 Class 3 · 10 MW_

## Overview

Cable fire class shall be B2ca-s1,d0 (Euroclass) per EN 13501-6 for data centre plenum areas.

## Requirements

- **CABLE-N1** — fire rating: shall be **B2ca-s1-d0 euroclass** (ref: EN-13501; clause NE-8.1)
- **CABLE-N1** — category: shall be **Cat6A type** (ref: EN-50600; clause NE-8.2)
