# Project Nordic Edge — Design Basis: Cooling — Free Cooling Dominant
_System: COOL · EN 50600 Class 3 · 10 MW_

## Overview

Oslo climate enables >7000 free cooling hours/year. Design PUE target 1.15 per EU Code of Conduct for Data Centres.

## Requirements

- **COOL-N1** — free cooling hours: shall be **7000 h/yr** (ref: DESIGN-BASIS; clause NE-6.1)
- **COOL-N1** — redundancy: shall be **N+1 topology** (ref: EN-50600; clause NE-6.2)
- **COOL-N1** — pue target: shall be **1.15 ratio** (ref: EU-COC; clause NE-6.3)
