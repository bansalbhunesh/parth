# Project Nordic Edge — Design Basis: Backup Generators
_System: GEN · EN 50600 Class 3 · 10 MW_

## Overview

Generators shall comply with Norwegian noise standard NS 8175 for urban environments (max 75 dB(A) at property boundary).

## Requirements

- **GEN-N1** — redundancy: shall be **N+1 topology** (ref: EN-50600; clause NE-5.1)
- **GEN-N1** — rated power kva: shall be **1500 kVA** (ref: DESIGN-BASIS; clause NE-5.2)
- **GEN-N1** — noise db: shall be **75 dB(A)** (ref: NS-8175; clause NE-5.3)
- **GEN-FUEL** — onsite fuel hours: shall be **48 h** (ref: DESIGN-BASIS; clause NE-5.4)
