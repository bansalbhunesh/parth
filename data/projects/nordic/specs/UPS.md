# Project Nordic Edge — Design Basis: Uninterruptible Power Supply
_System: UPS · EN 50600 Class 3 · 10 MW_

## Overview

UPS with lithium-ion batteries for reduced footprint and longer cycle life in cold climate.

## Requirements

- **UPS-N1** — battery runtime min: shall be **10 min** (ref: EN-50600; clause NE-4.1)
- **UPS-N1** — redundancy: shall be **N+1 topology** (ref: EN-50600; clause NE-4.2)
- **UPS-N1** — rated power kw: shall be **500 kW** (ref: DESIGN-BASIS; clause NE-4.3)
- **UPS-N1** — battery type: shall be **Li-ion type** (ref: DESIGN-BASIS; clause NE-4.4)
