# Project Nordic Edge — Design Basis Document

10 MW EN 50600 Class 3 data centre, Oslo, Norway.

- UPS: 500 kW Li-ion, N+1
- Generators: 1500 kVA, N+1, 75 dB(A) max
- Cooling: indirect evaporative, 7000 h/yr free cooling, PUE 1.15
- Cable: B2ca-s1,d0 Euroclass
- On-site fuel: 48 hours (extended for remote Norwegian grid)
