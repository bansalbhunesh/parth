# EN 13501-6 — Fire Classification of Cables (paraphrased)

Euroclass system for cable fire performance:
- Aca: non-combustible
- B1ca / B2ca: limited combustibility (required for data centres)
- Cca: limited fire spread
- Dca / Eca: flammable
Suffix s1/s2/s3 = smoke, d0/d1/d2 = flaming droplets.
B2ca-s1,d0 minimum for plenum/riser areas in data centres.
