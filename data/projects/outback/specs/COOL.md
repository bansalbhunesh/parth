# Project Outback — Design Basis: Cooling Plant
_System: COOL · Uptime Tier III · 8 MW_

## Overview

Cooling water efficiency shall achieve NABERS 5-star target of <= 3.0 L/kWh for Australian water conservation.

## Requirements

- **COOL-01** — redundancy: shall be **N+1 topology** (ref: UPTIME-TIER3; clause OB-6.1)
- **COOL-01** — water efficiency: shall be **3.0 L/kWh** (ref: NABERS-DC; clause OB-6.2)
- **COOL-01** — capacity tr: shall be **400 TR** (ref: DESIGN-BASIS; clause OB-6.3)
