# Project Outback — Design Basis: Diesel Generators
_System: GEN · Uptime Tier III · 8 MW_

## Overview

Generator noise emissions shall not exceed 75 dBA at 1m per AS 1055 and local council DA conditions.

## Requirements

- **GEN-01** — redundancy: shall be **N+1 topology** (ref: UPTIME-TIER3; clause OB-5.1)
- **GEN-01** — rated power kva: shall be **1500 kVA** (ref: DESIGN-BASIS; clause OB-5.2)
- **GEN-01** — noise dba: shall be **75 dBA** (ref: AS-1055; clause OB-5.3)
