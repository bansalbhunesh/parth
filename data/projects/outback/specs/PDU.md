# Project Outback — Design Basis: Power Distribution Units
_System: PDU · Uptime Tier III · 8 MW_

## Overview

PDU residual current devices shall be Type B per AS/NZS 3000 amendment for DC leakage detection from modern server PSUs.

## Requirements

- **PDU-01** — metering: shall be **per_outlet type** (ref: DESIGN-BASIS; clause OB-11.1)
- **PDU-01** — rated current a: shall be **32 A** (ref: AS-3000; clause OB-11.2)
- **PDU-01** — rcd type: shall be **Type_B class** (ref: AS-3000; clause OB-11.3)
