# Project Pampas — Design Basis: Diesel Generators
_System: GEN · Uptime Tier II · 6 MW_

## Overview

Generators shall operate on diesel B12 blend (12% biodiesel) per ABNT NBR 15940 and ANP Resolution 45.

## Requirements

- **GEN-01** — redundancy: shall be **N+1 topology** (ref: UPTIME-TIER2; clause PA-5.1)
- **GEN-01** — rated power kva: shall be **1200 kVA** (ref: DESIGN-BASIS; clause PA-5.2)
- **GEN-01** — fuel type: shall be **diesel_B12 type** (ref: ABNT-NBR-15940; clause PA-5.3)
