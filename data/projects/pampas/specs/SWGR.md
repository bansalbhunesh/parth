# Project Pampas — Design Basis: MV Switchgear
_System: SWGR · Uptime Tier II · 6 MW_

## Overview

MV switchgear rated for 13.8 kV distribution per ABNT NBR 14039 with fault withstand per utility coordination study.

## Requirements

- **SWGR-01** — voltage kv: shall be **13.8 kV** (ref: ABNT-NBR-14039; clause PA-7.1)
- **SWGR-01** — short circuit rating ka: shall be **25 kA** (ref: DESIGN-BASIS; clause PA-7.2)
