# Project Sahara — Design Basis: Cabling
_System: CABLE · Uptime Tier II · 5 MW_

## Overview

Power cables shall be rated for 90 C conductor temperature per DEWA requirements for UAE climate.

## Requirements

- **CABLE-S1** — temp rating c: shall be **90 C** (ref: DEWA-REG; clause SH-8.1)
- **CABLE-S1** — category: shall be **Cat6A type** (ref: TIA-942; clause SH-8.2)
