# Project Sahara — Design Basis: Cooling — Desert Climate
_System: COOL · Uptime Tier II · 5 MW_

## Overview

Cooling system shall be rated for 50 C outdoor design ambient per DEWA regulation for critical infrastructure.

## Requirements

- **COOL-S1** — redundancy: shall be **N+1 topology** (ref: UPTIME-TIER2; clause SH-6.1)
- **COOL-S1** — design ambient c: shall be **50 C** (ref: DEWA-REG; clause SH-6.2)
- **COOL-S1** — capacity tr: shall be **500 TR** (ref: DESIGN-BASIS; clause SH-6.3)
