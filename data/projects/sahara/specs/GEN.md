# Project Sahara — Design Basis: Diesel Generators
_System: GEN · Uptime Tier II · 5 MW_

## Overview

Generators shall be derated by 10% for ambient temperatures exceeding 50 C per DEWA regulations.

## Requirements

- **GEN-S1** — redundancy: shall be **N+1 topology** (ref: UPTIME-TIER2; clause SH-5.1)
- **GEN-S1** — rated power kva: shall be **1000 kVA** (ref: DESIGN-BASIS; clause SH-5.2)
- **GEN-S1** — derating pct: shall be **10 %** (ref: DEWA-REG; clause SH-5.3)
