# Project Sakura — Design Basis: Cabling Infrastructure
_System: CABLE · JEITA Class 4 · 25 MW_

## Overview

Data cables shall be EM-IT rated (eco-material, IT grade) per JIS C 3521 for low smoke and halogen-free performance.

## Requirements

- **CABLE-01** — fire rating: shall be **EM-IT JIS-class** (ref: JIS-C-3521; clause SK-8.1)
- **CABLE-01** — category: shall be **Cat6A type** (ref: JIS-X-5150; clause SK-8.2)
