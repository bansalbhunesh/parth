# Project Sakura — Design Basis: Diesel Generators
_System: GEN · JEITA Class 4 · 25 MW_

## Overview

Generators shall be S-class seismically rated per JEAC 4601 for Tokyo seismic zone (peak ground acceleration 0.4g).

## Requirements

- **GEN-01** — redundancy: shall be **N+1 topology** (ref: JEITA-DC; clause SK-5.1)
- **GEN-01** — rated power kva: shall be **2500 kVA** (ref: DESIGN-BASIS; clause SK-5.2)
- **GEN-01** — seismic class: shall be **S_class class** (ref: JEAC-4601; clause SK-5.3)
