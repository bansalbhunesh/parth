# Project Sakura — Design Basis: Structural — Seismic Isolation
_System: STRUCT · JEITA Class 4 · 25 MW_

## Overview

Data hall structure shall incorporate base isolation per JEAC 4601 to achieve 0.2g floor response for seismic zone Tokyo.

## Requirements

- **FLOOR-01** — seismic isolation: shall be **base_isolated type** (ref: JEAC-4601; clause SK-9.1)
- **FLOOR-01** — load rating kpa: shall be **15 kPa** (ref: DESIGN-BASIS; clause SK-9.2)
