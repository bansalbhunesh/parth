# Project Sakura — Design Basis: Uninterruptible Power Supply
_System: UPS · JEITA Class 4 · 25 MW_

## Requirements

- **UPS-01** — battery runtime min: shall be **10 min** (ref: JEITA-DC; clause SK-4.1)
- **UPS-01** — redundancy: shall be **2N topology** (ref: JEITA-DC; clause SK-4.2)
- **UPS-01** — rated power kw: shall be **1000 kW** (ref: DESIGN-BASIS; clause SK-4.3)
