# Project Thames — Design Basis: Cooling Plant
_System: COOL · Uptime Tier IV · 35 MW_

## Overview

Cooling plant PUE target 1.3 using London's mild climate for extensive free cooling via indirect adiabatic economisers.

## Requirements

- **COOL-01** — redundancy: shall be **N+2 topology** (ref: UPTIME-TIER4; clause TH-6.1)
- **COOL-01** — pue target: shall be **1.3 ratio** (ref: DESIGN-BASIS; clause TH-6.2)
- **COOL-01** — capacity tr: shall be **1400 TR** (ref: DESIGN-BASIS; clause TH-6.3)
