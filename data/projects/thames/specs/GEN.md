# Project Thames — Design Basis: Diesel Generators
_System: GEN · Uptime Tier IV · 35 MW_

## Overview

Generators shall comply with Medium Combustion Plant Directive 2015/2193 NOx limits for urban London site.

## Requirements

- **GEN-01** — redundancy: shall be **N+1 topology** (ref: UPTIME-TIER4; clause TH-5.1)
- **GEN-01** — rated power kva: shall be **2750 kVA** (ref: DESIGN-BASIS; clause TH-5.2)
- **GEN-01** — emission nox gkwh: shall be **0.4 g/kWh** (ref: MCPD-2015/2193; clause TH-5.3)
