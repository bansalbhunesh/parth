# Project Vajra — Design Basis: Cooling Plant
_System: COOL · Uptime Tier III · 20 MW_

## Overview

Chilled water cooling plant with supply air temperature per ASHRAE TC 9.9 Class A1 recommended envelope (18-27 C, target 24 C).

## Requirements

- **COOL-01** — redundancy: shall be **N+1 topology** (ref: UPTIME-TIER3; clause VJ-6.1)
- **COOL-01** — supply air temp c: shall be **24 C** (ref: ASHRAE-TC9.9; clause VJ-6.2)
- **COOL-01** — capacity tr: shall be **800 TR** (ref: DESIGN-BASIS; clause VJ-6.3)
