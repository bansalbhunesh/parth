# Project Vajra — Design Basis: Diesel Generators
_System: GEN · Uptime Tier III · 20 MW_

## Overview

Standby generators with N+1 redundancy. Start time shall not exceed 10 seconds per Tier III.

## Requirements

- **GEN-01** — redundancy: shall be **N+1 topology** (ref: UPTIME-TIER3; clause VJ-5.1)
- **GEN-01** — rated power kva: shall be **2000 kVA** (ref: DESIGN-BASIS; clause VJ-5.2)
- **GEN-01** — start time sec: shall be **10 s** (ref: UPTIME-TIER3; clause VJ-5.3)
- **GEN-FUEL** — onsite fuel hours: shall be **24 h** (ref: UPTIME-TIER3; clause VJ-5.4)
