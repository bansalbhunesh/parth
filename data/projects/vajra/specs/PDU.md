# Project Vajra — Design Basis: Power Distribution Units
_System: PDU · Uptime Tier III · 20 MW_

## Overview

Rack PDUs shall provide per-outlet metering for capacity planning and PUE reporting.

## Requirements

- **PDU-01** — metering: shall be **per_outlet type** (ref: DESIGN-BASIS; clause VJ-9.1)
- **PDU-01** — redundancy: shall be **A+B topology** (ref: UPTIME-TIER3; clause VJ-9.2)
- **PDU-01** — rated current a: shall be **32 A** (ref: DESIGN-BASIS; clause VJ-9.3)
