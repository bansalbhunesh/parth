# Project Vajra — Design Basis: Uninterruptible Power Supply
_System: UPS · Uptime Tier III · 20 MW_

## Overview

UPS system shall provide conditioned power with N+1 redundancy for concurrent maintainability per Tier III design basis.

## Requirements

- **UPS-01** — battery runtime min: shall be **10 min** (ref: UPTIME-TIER3; clause VJ-4.1)
- **UPS-01** — redundancy: shall be **N+1 topology** (ref: UPTIME-TIER3; clause VJ-4.2)
- **UPS-01** — rated power kw: shall be **800 kW** (ref: DESIGN-BASIS; clause VJ-4.3)
- **UPS-01** — efficiency pct: shall be **96 %** (ref: DESIGN-BASIS; clause VJ-4.4)
