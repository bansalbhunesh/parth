# Uptime Tier III — Concurrently Maintainable (paraphrased summary)

Tier III requires that every capacity component and distribution element can be removed from service for planned maintenance without impacting IT operations.

## Key requirements
- Power distribution: N+1 minimum
- Cooling: N+1 minimum
- Battery autonomy: minimum 10 minutes
- Generator start time: <= 10 seconds
- On-site fuel: 24 hours minimum
- Availability target: 99.982%
