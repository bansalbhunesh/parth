# Project Yangtze — Design Basis: Building Management & Government Reporting
_System: BMS · GB 50174 Grade A · 50 MW_

## Overview

BMS shall connect to municipal fire bureau reporting system per GB 50174 and report PUE to MIIT in real time.

## Requirements

- **BMS-Y1** — protocol: shall be **BACnet_IP standard** (ref: DESIGN-BASIS; clause YZ-9.1)
- **BMS-Y1** — gov fire reporting: shall be **connected status** (ref: GB-50174; clause YZ-9.2)
- **BMS-Y1** — pue reporting: shall be **real_time type** (ref: MIIT-REG; clause YZ-9.3)
