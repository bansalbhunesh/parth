# Project Yangtze — Design Basis: Cooling — Liquid Cooling Hybrid
_System: COOL · GB 50174 Grade A · 50 MW_

## Overview

High-density design with 30 kW/rack average, 60% direct liquid cooling for GPU clusters.

## Requirements

- **COOL-Y1** — redundancy: shall be **N+2 topology** (ref: GB-50174; clause YZ-6.1)
- **COOL-Y1** — rack power kw: shall be **30 kW** (ref: DESIGN-BASIS; clause YZ-6.2)
- **COOL-Y1** — liquid cooling pct: shall be **60 %** (ref: DESIGN-BASIS; clause YZ-6.3)
