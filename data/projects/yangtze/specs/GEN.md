# Project Yangtze — Design Basis: Diesel Generators
_System: GEN · GB 50174 Grade A · 50 MW_

## Requirements

- **GEN-Y1** — redundancy: shall be **N+1 topology** (ref: GB-50174; clause YZ-5.1)
- **GEN-Y1** — rated power kva: shall be **3000 kVA** (ref: DESIGN-BASIS; clause YZ-5.2)
- **GEN-FUEL** — onsite fuel hours: shall be **24 h** (ref: GB-50174; clause YZ-5.4)
