# Project Yangtze — Design Basis: Uninterruptible Power Supply
_System: UPS · GB 50174 Grade A · 50 MW_

## Overview

GB 50174 Grade A requires minimum 15 minutes battery for facilities >10 MW with dual utility feeds.

## Requirements

- **UPS-Y1** — battery runtime min: shall be **15 min** (ref: GB-50174; clause YZ-4.1)
- **UPS-Y1** — redundancy: shall be **2N topology** (ref: GB-50174; clause YZ-4.2)
- **UPS-Y1** — rated power kw: shall be **1500 kW** (ref: DESIGN-BASIS; clause YZ-4.3)
