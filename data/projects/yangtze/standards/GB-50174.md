# GB 50174-2017 — Code for Design of Data Centres (paraphrased)

Chinese national standard for data centre design. Three grades:
- Grade A: highest availability, equivalent to Tier III+/IV
- Grade B: moderate availability
- Grade C: basic

## Grade A requirements
- UPS: 2N topology, >= 15 min battery for >10 MW
- Cooling: N+2 redundancy
- Generator: N+1, 24h fuel
- Fire: connected to municipal fire bureau reporting
- PUE: reported to MIIT in real time for facilities >5000 racks
