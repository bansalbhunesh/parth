# DESIGN BASIS — STANDBY DIESEL GENERATOR
## Project Helios Data Centre · Emergency Power · Issued for Tender (Rev A)

### 1. SCOPE

This section governs the standby diesel generating set(s) providing emergency
power to the critical and life-safety loads of the Helios data centre on loss of
utility supply. The generator subsystem shall comply with this design basis and
with the applicable emissions, fire, and electrical regulations for the site.
Where a vendor proposal differs from any requirement below, it shall be recorded
as a deviation and is non-conforming unless approved in writing by the Engineer.

### 2. PERFORMANCE REQUIREMENTS

| Ref | Parameter | Requirement |
|-----|-----------|-------------|
| 2.1 | Standby rated power | ≥ **2000 kW** at site conditions |
| 2.2 | Output voltage / frequency | **11 kV, 50 Hz**, 3-phase |
| 2.3 | Emissions compliance | **EPA Tier 4 Final** (or EU Stage V) certified |
| 2.4 | On-site fuel autonomy | **≥ 24 hours** at 100% rated load without refuelling |
| 2.5 | Start & load acceptance | Reach **full rated load within 10 seconds** of a start signal |
| 2.6 | Redundancy | **N+1** generating capacity |
| 2.7 | Acoustic enclosure | ≤ **85 dB(A)** at 1 m |
| 2.8 | Governor | Electronic isochronous, ≤ ±0.25% steady-state |

### 3. NOTES TO TENDERER

- **Emissions (2.3):** the site requires Tier 4 Final / Stage V certification.
  Lower tiers (Tier 2, Stage IIIA) require aftertreatment or a formal variance
  and shall be treated as non-conforming.
- **Fuel (2.4):** state usable on-site storage at 100% load. A sub-base "belly"
  tank sized for only a few hours does not satisfy the 24-hour requirement.
- **Redundancy (2.6):** a single set does not satisfy N+1; provide a parallel
  set or an installed standby unit.
