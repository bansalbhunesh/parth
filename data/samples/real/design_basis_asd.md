# Design Basis — Aspirating Smoke Detection (Data Hall)
_Project Helios DC-2 · Very-early-warning fire detection · Rev A_
_Document DB-HELIOS-ASD · Issued for Tender_

This section governs the aspirating smoke detection (ASD) system for Data
Hall 1. The hall is a **single 1,600 m² fire detection zone** served by **one
ASD detector** (a second unit is the N+1 spare, not additional coverage). The
governing case is **very early warning** in a high-airflow environment.
Requirements derive from **EN 54-20** and NFPA 76 VEWFD practice. A submittal
providing less shall be recorded as a non-conformance.

## 1. Coverage / sensitivity

- **Coverage per detector.** One detector shall cover the full
  **1,600 m²** hall zone.
- **Sensitivity.** Detector sensitivity range shall extend down to
  **0.005 % obscuration/m** (very-early-warning class).
- **Listing.** EN 54-20 listed aspirating detector.

## 2. Pipe network / interface

- **Sampling network.** Engineered pipe network per the manufacturer's
  aspirator design tool, covering the ceiling void and supply-air path.
- **Interfaces.** Relay outputs to the fire alarm control panel; alarm
  staging (Alert / Action / Fire 1 / Fire 2).
- **Power.** 24 V dc from the monitored fire-power supply.
