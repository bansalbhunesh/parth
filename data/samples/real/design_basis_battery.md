# Design Basis — UPS Battery System
_Project Helios DC-2 · Uptime Tier IV · EUROBAT / IEEE 1188_
_Document DB-HELIOS-7 · Rev A · Issued for Tender_

Stationary battery energy for the static UPS. Vendor proposals differing from
any requirement below shall be logged as non-conformances.

## 1. Battery System (per UPS module)

- **Design life.** Batteries shall have a design life of at least **10 years** at
  25 degrees C (EUROBAT "High Performance"/"Long Life" class).
- **Autonomy.** At least **10 minutes** at full rated load.
- **Monitoring.** Per-string battery monitoring (voltage, temperature, internal
  resistance) per IEEE 1188.
