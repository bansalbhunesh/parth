# Design Basis — Critical-Power Busway (Riser)
_Project Helios DC-2 · LV busbar trunking · IEC site · Rev A_
_Document DB-HELIOS-BUSWAY · Issued for Tender_

This section governs the low-voltage busbar trunking (busway) feeding the
critical-power riser between the LV switchboard and the floor PDUs. The governing
case is the **prospective short-circuit current** at the point of installation;
the trunking must withstand the fault for the protective-device clearing time
without failure. Requirements derive from **IEC 61439-6** (busbar trunking
systems). A submittal providing less shall be recorded as a non-conformance.

## 1. Electrical ratings

- **Rated current.** >= **1000 A** continuous at the design ambient.
- **Short-circuit withstand rating.** Rated short-time withstand current shall be
  >= **65 kA for 1 s**, matching the prospective fault level at the LV
  switchboard (IEC 61439-6).
- **Rated peak withstand current.** >= **105 kA**.
- **Ingress protection.** >= **IP54** in the service gallery.
- **Conductor.** Copper.

## 2. Fire / installation

- **Fire barrier.** Fire-rated barrier at every floor/wall penetration
  (IEC 61439-6 fire-stop provisions).
- **Earthing.** Integral PE conductor, full-length.
