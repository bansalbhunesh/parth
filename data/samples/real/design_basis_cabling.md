# Design Basis — Structured Cabling
_Project Helios DC-2 · Uptime Tier IV · NFPA 75 / TIA-942_
_Document DB-HELIOS-9 · Rev A · Issued for Tender_

Structured cabling for the data halls and plenum return-air spaces. Vendor
proposals differing from any requirement below shall be logged as
non-conformances.

## 1. Cabling

- **Plenum fire rating.** Cable installed in plenum / return-air spaces shall be
  **CMP (plenum-rated, UL 910)** per NFPA 75 / NFPA 262.
- **Copper.** **Category 6A** balanced twisted-pair.
- **Fibre.** **OM4** multimode / **OS2** singlemode.
