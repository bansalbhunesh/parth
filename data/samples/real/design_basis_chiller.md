# Design Basis — Water-Cooled Chiller Plant
_Project Helios DC-2 · Uptime Tier IV · ASHRAE 90.1 · EU F-Gas_
_Document DB-HELIOS-6 · Rev A · Issued for Tender_

Central water-cooled chiller plant serving the cooling loop. Vendor proposals
differing from any requirement below shall be logged as non-conformances.

## 1. Water-Cooled Chillers

- **Refrigerant.** Chiller refrigerant shall have a **Global Warming Potential
  (GWP) of 750 or less**, per the site sustainability standard aligned with the
  EU F-Gas phase-down.
- **Part-load efficiency.** Integrated Part Load Value (IPLV) shall be at least
  **0.55 kW/ton** or better (ASHRAE 90.1 path).
- **Redundancy.** The chiller plant shall be **N+1** at the design heat-rejection
  load.
- **Rated cooling capacity.** Each chiller shall provide at least **1,200 tons**
  at design conditions.
