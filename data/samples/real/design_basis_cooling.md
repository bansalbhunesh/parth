# Design Basis — Precision Cooling
_Project Helios DC-2 · Uptime Tier IV · ASHRAE TC9.9 Class A1 · EU-regulated site_
_Document DB-HELIOS-3 · Rev A · Issued for Tender_

This section governs the computer-room air conditioning (CRAC/CRAH) units
serving the data halls. Where a vendor proposal differs from any requirement
below, it shall be recorded as a non-conformance and resolved before equipment
release. Vendor part-load or economy figures are not credited toward a
requirement unless explicitly stated.

## 1. Computer-Room Cooling Units (per cooling cell)

- **Redundancy.** Room cooling shall be provided at **N+2** redundancy, such that
  the full design heat load is maintained with any two units out of service
  concurrently (Uptime Tier IV fault tolerance and concurrent maintainability).
- **Supply-air temperature.** Unit supply air shall be controlled within the
  ASHRAE TC9.9 Class A1 recommended envelope and shall not exceed **27 degrees C**
  at the rack inlet.
- **Fans.** Units shall use **EC (electronically commutated) variable-speed**
  plug fans for part-load efficiency.
- **Refrigerant (DX units).** Any direct-expansion refrigerant shall have a
  Global Warming Potential (GWP) of **750 or less**, per the site sustainability
  standard aligned with the EU F-Gas phase-down away from high-GWP HFCs
  (Regulation (EU) 517/2014 and its 2024 revision).
- **Net sensible cooling.** Each cooling cell shall provide a net sensible
  cooling capacity of at least **200 kW** at the design return-air condition.
