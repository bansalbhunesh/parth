# Design Basis — Clean-Agent Fire Suppression
_Project Helios DC-2 · Uptime Tier IV · NFPA 2001 · EU F-Gas / US AIM Act_
_Document DB-HELIOS-5 · Rev A · Issued for Tender_

Total-flooding clean-agent suppression for the data halls. Where a vendor
proposal differs from any requirement below, it shall be recorded as a
non-conformance and resolved before equipment release.

## 1. Clean-Agent System (per protected volume)

- **Agent environmental profile.** The clean agent shall have a **Global Warming
  Potential (GWP) of 750 or less**, to remain compliant through the HFC phase-down
  (EU F-Gas Regulation and the US AIM Act) over the system's 15–20 year life.
- **Discharge time.** Agent shall reach design concentration in **10 seconds or
  less** (NFPA 2001).
- **Design concentration.** Per the listed agent, with a safety margin above the
  minimum extinguishing concentration (NFPA 2001).
- **Electrical safety.** Agent shall be electrically non-conductive and safe for
  energised electronics.
