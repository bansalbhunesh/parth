# Design Basis — Raised Access Floor (Critical IT Hall)
_Project Helios DC-2 · High-density AI/GPU hall · Uptime Tier III+ · US site_
_Document DB-HELIOS-FLOOR · Rev A · Issued for Tender_

This section governs the structural raised access floor in the critical IT hall.
The hall is designed for fully-populated high-density racks; a 42U AI/GPU rack
with its PDU and cabling routinely exceeds 1400 kg, and the governing load case
is the **concentrated point load** under a leveling foot or caster during
install and equipment moves. Loads below are minimums; a submittal that provides
less shall be recorded as a non-conformance and resolved before release.

## 1. Panel structural performance (CISCA test methods)

- **Concentrated load.** Each panel, supported on its actual understructure,
  shall sustain a concentrated point load of at least **1500 lbf** applied on a
  one-square-inch area at any location, without permanent set (CISCA
  "Recommended Test Procedures for Access Floors", Concentrated Load method).
  This corresponds to a Tate ConCore **1500**-class panel or equivalent.
- **Ultimate load.** Minimum **3000 lbf** point load before failure (CISCA
  Ultimate Loading — safety factor >= 2 on the design load).
- **Rolling load.** Wheel 2 >= **800 lb** for 10000 passes without local/overall
  deformation greater than 0.040 in (CISCA Rolling Loads) — required for repeated
  equipment moves.
- **Pedestal axial load.** Understructure pedestal assembly >= **6000 lbf** axial
  without permanent deformation (CISCA Pedestal Axial Load).

## 2. System / material

- **Combustibility.** All access-floor components shall qualify as
  non-combustible per **ASTM E136**.
- **Flame spread.** Floor covering system shall meet **Class A** flame-spread and
  smoke-development per **ASTM E84**.
- **Understructure.** Bolted-stringer grid, seismically braced per the project
  structural criteria (ASCE 7 / IBC).
- **Seismic design.** Seismically qualified for **Zone IV per IS 1893 (Part 1): 2016** with an Importance Factor (I) of **1.5** (critical infrastructure post-disaster functionality).
