# Design Basis — Lithium-Ion UPS Battery Room (Energy Storage)
_Project Helios DC-2 · Stationary ESS for critical UPS · Rev A_
_Document DB-HELIOS-ESS · Issued for Tender_

This section governs the lithium-ion battery system for the critical UPS
plant, installed in **Battery Room B-1, a single fire area** protected by the
prescriptive sprinkler design (0.3 gpm/ft² over the design area). The room and
its protection are engineered to the **NFPA 855 prescriptive path** — the
listed limits below are hard limits; exceeding any of them re-opens the fire
protection design. A submittal exceeding them shall be recorded as a
non-conformance.

## 1. NFPA 855 prescriptive limits (Battery Room B-1)

- **Maximum stored energy per ESS unit (rack/cabinet).** <= **50 kWh**.
- **Separation.** >= **914 mm (3 ft)** between ESS units and from walls.
- **Maximum aggregate stored energy in the fire area.** <= **600 kWh**
  of lithium-ion ESS in Battery Room B-1 (single fire area; no subdivision
  is provided in the base build).

## 2. Listings / test data

- **UL 9540** listed ESS; cells per UL 1973.
- **UL 9540A test data** (cell, module, and unit/rack level) shall be
  submitted with the proposal — required for fire-protection review even
  within the prescriptive limits.

## 3. Interface

- Battery management system with per-module monitoring, integrated to the
  UPS controls and the BMS head-end; dry-contact alarm to the FACP.
