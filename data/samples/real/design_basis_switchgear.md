# Design Basis — Low-Voltage Switchgear
_Project Helios DC-2 · Uptime Tier IV · IEC 61439-2_
_Document DB-HELIOS-4 · Rev A · Issued for Tender_

This section governs the main low-voltage (LV) switchgear assembly on the
critical power path. Where a vendor proposal differs from any requirement below,
it shall be recorded as a non-conformance and resolved before equipment release.
Ratings shall be type-tested or design-verified per IEC 61439-2; vendor optional
variants are not credited unless explicitly included in the proposal.

## 1. Main LV Switchgear Assembly

- **Short-circuit withstand.** The assembly shall have a rated short-time
  withstand current (Icw) of at least **65 kA for 1 second** (IEC 61439-2),
  coordinated with the prospective fault level at the main LV board.
- **Internal separation.** The assembly shall provide **Form 4b** internal
  separation between busbars, functional units, and outgoing terminals
  (IEC 61439-2).
- **Internal arc containment.** The assembly shall be arc-resistant and
  type-tested for internal arcing faults to **IEC 61641** (IAC classification)
  at the rated fault level.
- **Ingress protection.** Enclosure protection shall be at least **IP42** for the
  indoor switchroom.
- **Rated operational voltage.** 415 V, three-phase, 50 Hz.
