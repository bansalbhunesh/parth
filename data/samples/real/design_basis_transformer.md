# Design Basis — Dry-Type Distribution Transformer
_Project Helios DC-2 · Uptime Tier IV · IEC 60076-11_
_Document DB-HELIOS-8 · Rev A · Issued for Tender_

Cast-resin dry-type transformers on the critical distribution. Vendor proposals
differing from any requirement below shall be logged as non-conformances.

## 1. Dry-Type Transformer

- **Harmonic rating.** Transformer shall be **K-factor rated K-13** to tolerate
  the non-linear (UPS / rectifier) data-centre load without overheating.
- **Insulation class.** **Class F (155 degrees C)** insulation system, cast-resin.
- **Climatic / fire class.** **F1** (fire behaviour) per IEC 60076-11.
- **Vector group.** **Dyn11**.
- **Impedance.** **6%** at rated load.
