# Design Basis — Auxiliary / IT-Closet Cooling
_Project Meridian DC-3 · Telecom/IT closet supplemental cooling · US site_
_Document DB-MERIDIAN-4 · Rev A · Issued for Tender_

This section governs supplemental cooling for telecom and IT closets outside
the main data-hall CRAH plant (see `../design_basis_cooling.md` for the
main-plant requirement). Where a vendor proposal differs from any
requirement below, it shall be recorded as a non-conformance and resolved
before equipment release.

## 1. Auxiliary Cooling Unit

- **Refrigerant sustainability.** The refrigerant's Global Warming
  Potential (GWP) shall not exceed **750**, per the same site
  sustainability standard applied to the main cooling plant (EU F-Gas
  phase-down alignment). Where the refrigerant is stated by designation
  only, its GWP shall be verified against a primary regulatory source, not
  assumed — see `../primary_sources/40_CFR_98_Table_A-1_GWP.md`.
- **Capacity.** 2–5 tons per unit, ducted or ductless, suitable for a
  small IT/telecom closet outside the main data hall footprint.
- **Retrofit compatibility.** Where replacing an existing unit, the
  proposal shall state whether existing refrigerant piping can be reused.
