# Design Basis — Fire Pump Diesel Driver
_Project Meridian DC-3 · NFPA 20 fire pump room · US (EPA-regulated) site_
_Document DB-MERIDIAN-3 · Rev A · Issued for Tender_

This section governs the diesel engine driver for the fire pump serving the
suppression system's water supply. Where a vendor proposal differs from any
requirement below, it shall be recorded as a non-conformance and resolved
before equipment release.

## 1. Diesel Fire Pump Driver

- **Rated power class.** 56–75 kW (75–100 HP) constant-speed diesel engine,
  matched to the fire pump's rated brake horsepower per NFPA 20.
- **Engine exhaust emissions.** The engine shall be certified to the
  currently applicable federal stationary fire-pump-engine emission
  standard for its power class — **40 CFR Part 60, Subpart IIII, Table 4**
  (2011-and-later model year row for 56 ≤ kW < 75): **NMHC+NOx not to
  exceed 4.7 g/kW-hr (3.5 g/HP-hr); CO not to exceed 5.0 g/kW-hr
  (3.7 g/HP-hr); PM not to exceed 0.40 g/kW-hr (0.30 g/HP-hr)** — quoted
  verbatim from the stored primary-source text at
  `../primary_sources/40_CFR_60_Subpart_IIII.md` (lines 1036–1040 of that
  file), not paraphrased. This is the current federal floor for this power
  class, not a project-specific stricter target — unlike the Tier 4
  requirement in `../design_basis_helios.md`, which the primary-source
  addendum there identifies as stricter than code.
- **Certification basis.** Compliance shall be demonstrated by an EPA
  emissions-certificate reference (Family Name and Certificate Number) for
  the exact engine family proposed, not a general tier claim.
