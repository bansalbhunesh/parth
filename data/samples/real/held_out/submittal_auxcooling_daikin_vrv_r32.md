# Vendor Submittal — Auxiliary / IT-Closet Cooling
_Project Meridian DC-3 · Submittal SUB-MERIDIAN-4 · Rev A_

The proposed auxiliary cooling unit is a Daikin VRV S-series R-32 system
(outdoor unit RXTA).

## 1. Auxiliary Cooling Unit — Daikin VRV S-series R-32 (RXTA)

- **Refrigerant.** **R-32 (HFC-32) exclusively** — confirmed by the
  product line name and Daikin's own product page (daikincomfort.com,
  "VRV S-series R-32"). Per the stored primary-source GWP table
  (`../primary_sources/40_CFR_98_Table_A-1_GWP.md`, CAS 75-10-5), HFC-32's
  100-year GWP is **677**.
- **Capacity.** 2–5 tons (up to 60,000 Btu/hr on the 5-ton model).
- **Piping.** Vertical separation up to 164 ft; total pipe run up to
  984 ft.
- **Noise.** As low as 56 dB(A).
- **Retrofit.** Can replace an existing R-410A system while reusing
  larger-diameter refrigerant piping via field settings, per the
  manufacturer's published retrofit guidance.
