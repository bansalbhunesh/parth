# Vendor Submittal — Low-Voltage Switchgear
_Project Helios DC-2 · Submittal SUB-HELIOS-4 · Rev A_

Proposed: ABB MNS low-voltage switchgear, design-verified to IEC 61439-1/-2.

## 1. Main LV Switchgear — ABB MNS

- **Short-circuit withstand.** Rated short-time withstand current (Icw) of
  **50 kA for 1 second** in the proposed configuration.
- **Internal separation.** **Form 3b** internal separation.
- **Internal arc containment.** Standard construction. Internal-arc type testing
  to IEC 61641 is available as the MNS arc-proof variant but is **not included**
  in this proposal.
- **Ingress protection.** Enclosure rated **IP54**.
- **Rated operational voltage.** 415 V, three-phase, 50 Hz.
