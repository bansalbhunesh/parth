# Vendor Submittal — BMS Network Controller
_Project Helios DC-2 · Submittal SUB-HELIOS-BMSC · Rev A_

Proposed controller: **Distech Controls ECB-600** programmable controller
(28 points). All figures are Distech's published ECB series specifications.

## 1. BACnet conformance — ECB-600

- **Device profile.** BTL-listed **B-AAC (BACnet Advanced Application
  Controller)**.
- **Transport.** **BACnet MS/TP LAN** communication.

## 2. Control functions

- **Programmability.** Fully programmable (GFX/EC-gfxProgram), 28-point
  I/O mix for advanced HVAC and plant applications.
- **Scheduling / trending / alarming.** Application-level schedules, trends,
  and alarms supported within the AAC profile's BIBB set.
- **Integration.** Operates as a field device under a supervisory
  BACnet/IP network controller or head-end providing IP routing.
