# Vendor Submittal — Critical-Power Busway
_Project Helios DC-2 · Submittal SUB-HELIOS-BUSWAY · Rev A_

Proposed system: **Schneider Electric Canalis KTA10** (1000 A) high-power busbar
trunking, standard (non-reinforced) construction. All figures are Schneider's
published ratings (Canalis KTA 800–4000 A catalogue, I_cw / I_pk table, standard
3L + PE version).

## 1. Electrical ratings — Canalis KTA10

- **Rated current.** **1000 A** continuous.
- **Short-circuit withstand rating.** Rated short-time withstand current
  **50 kA for 1 s** (standard KTA10 construction; the reinforced 3L+N+PER variant
  is required to reach 65 kA).
- **Rated peak withstand current.** **110 kA**.
- **Ingress protection.** **IP55** (exceeds IP54).
- **Conductor.** Copper.

## 2. Fire / installation

- **Fire barrier.** Fire-rated fire-stop blocks at floor penetrations
  (Canalis KS/KT fire-barrier accessory), IEC 61439-6.
- **Earthing.** Integral PE, full-length.
- Tested to **IEC 61439-6**.
