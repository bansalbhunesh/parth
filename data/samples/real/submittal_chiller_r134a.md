# Vendor Submittal — Water-Cooled Chiller Plant
_Project Helios DC-2 · Submittal SUB-HELIOS-6 · Rev A_

Proposed: water-cooled centrifugal chillers (Carrier 19-series class).

## 1. Water-Cooled Centrifugal Chillers

- **Refrigerant.** **R-134a**.
- **Part-load efficiency.** IPLV **0.50 kW/ton**.
- **Redundancy.** **N+1** at design load.
- **Rated capacity.** **1,250 tons** per chiller at design conditions.
