# Vendor Submittal — Precision Cooling Setpoint
_Project Helios DC-2 · Submittal SUB-HELIOS-THERMAL · Rev A_

Proposed control narrative for the precision cooling units (EC-fan CRAH).

## 1. Thermal setpoint

- **Supply-air temperature.** Units controlled to a **30 °C** rack-inlet supply
  setpoint to maximise chiller economiser / free-cooling hours and reduce annual
  PUE. This value sits **within the ASHRAE TC 9.9 Class A1 _allowable_ range
  (15–32 °C)** though **above the recommended 27 °C**.
- **Humidity.** Maintained within A1 allowable.
- **Fans.** EC variable-speed plug fans.
- **Redundancy.** N+1.

> The 30 °C setpoint is proposed as an energy-efficiency optimisation, with the
> note that it remains inside ASHRAE A1 allowable limits.
