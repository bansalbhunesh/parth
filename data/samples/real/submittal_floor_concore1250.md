# Vendor Submittal — Raised Access Floor
_Project Helios DC-2 · Submittal SUB-HELIOS-FLOOR · Rev A_

Proposed system: **Tate ConCore 1250** cementitious-filled welded steel access
floor panel on a bolted-stringer understructure. All figures below are Tate's
published, CISCA-certified ratings (Tate ConCore 1250 specification, Section
09 69 00, R07/15).

## 1. Panel — Tate ConCore 1250

- **Concentrated load.** Rated concentrated design load of **1250 lbf** on a
  one-square-inch area at any location, without permanent set, tested on the
  actual understructure (CISCA Concentrated Load method).
- **Ultimate load.** At least **2500 lbf** point load before failure (CISCA
  Ultimate Loading — safety factor of 2 on the 1250 lbf design load).
- **Rolling load.** Wheel 1: 1000 lb, 10 passes. Wheel 2: **800 lb, 10000
  passes** — deformation within 0.040 in (CISCA Rolling Loads).
- **Pedestal axial load.** **6000 lbf** axial without permanent deformation
  (CISCA Pedestal Axial Load Test).
- **Stringer concentrated load.** 450 lbf at midspan, permanent set <= 0.010 in.

## 2. System / material

- **Combustibility.** All components qualify as non-combustible per **ASTM E136**
  (vertical tube furnace, 750 °C).
- **Flame spread.** Encapsulated high-pressure-laminate covering meets **Class A**
  per **ASTM E84**.
- **Understructure.** Bolted-stringer grid, 4-ft basketweave; corrosion-protected
  electro-deposited epoxy panels.
- Manufacturing: ISO 9001 / ISO 14001 certified facility.
- **Seismic design.** Tate bolted-stringer understructure is seismically braced for IBC Seismic Design Category C. Local compliance certification for **IS 1893 Zone IV** with Importance Factor I=1.5 is **not certified** / pending project-specific structural design.
