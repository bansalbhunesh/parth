# Vendor Submittal — Clean-Agent Fire Suppression
_Project Helios DC-2 · Submittal SUB-HELIOS-5 · Rev A_

Proposed: an **FM-200 (HFC-227ea)** total-flooding clean-agent system.

## 1. Clean-Agent System

- **Agent.** **FM-200 (HFC-227ea)**, electrically non-conductive, UL-listed.
- **Discharge time.** Reaches design concentration in **10 seconds**.
- **Design concentration.** **7%** by volume for Class A surface fire.
- **Cylinders.** Fewer storage cylinders than alternative agents for the same
  protected volume.
