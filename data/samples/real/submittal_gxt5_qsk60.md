# Vendor Submittal — Critical Power Package
_Project Helios DC-2 · Submittal SUB-HELIOS-2 · Rev B_

The proposed package comprises Vertiv Liebert GXT5 static UPS modules and a
Cummins QSK60 standby generating set. All figures below are the manufacturers'
published ratings.

## 1. Static UPS — Vertiv Liebert GXT5 (GXT5-1000LVRT2UXL)

Online double-conversion topology, 120 V, pure sine-wave output, internal
automatic bypass, Energy Star 2.0.

- **Battery autonomy.** The standard internal battery provides approximately
  **7 minutes** at full rated load (per the published GXT5 runtime table), and
  about 18 minutes at half load. Extended runtime is available via external
  battery cabinets (not included in this scope).
- **Efficiency.** Up to **99 percent** in Active ECO mode. In continuous online
  double-conversion mode, efficiency is **95.9 percent** at full load.
- **Output power factor.** **0.9 to 1.0** (unity-capable output).
- **Input THD.** Total harmonic distortion figures are available on request.

## 2. Standby Generator — Cummins QSK60-G6 (2000 kW standby)

60.2-litre V-16 (QSK60-G6 NR2), 277/480 V, 60 Hz, NFPA 110 Level 1 listed,
UL 2200 listed.

- **Start time.** NFPA 110 Type 10 capable — starts and accepts load within
  **10 seconds**.
- **Engine emissions.** **EPA Tier 2** certified.
- **Fuel.** Proposed **4,000-gallon** day tank. Engine fuel consumption is
  **103 gallons per hour** at 100 percent load.
