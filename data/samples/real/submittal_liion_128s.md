# Vendor Submittal — Lithium-Ion UPS Battery System
_Project Helios DC-2 · Submittal SUB-HELIOS-ESS · Rev A_

Proposed system: **Samsung SDI 128S** lithium-ion battery racks (UPS
application). All figures are Samsung SDI's published product-specification
values; the 128S/136S family was the **first lithium-ion battery cabinet to
complete the UL 9540A rack-level test**.

## 1. Proposed configuration — Battery Room B-1

- **Rack count.** **24 racks** of Samsung SDI 128S, arranged in four rows.
- **Nameplate energy per rack.** **26.5 kWh** (128S configuration).
- **Separation.** Rows and racks laid out at **1,000 mm** clear separation
  from adjacent units and walls.

## 2. Listings / test data

- **UL 9540** listed system; cells/modules per **UL 1973**.
- **UL 9540A test data**: cell-, module-, and **rack-level** UL 9540A test
  reports are included with this submittal.

## 3. Interface

- Samsung SDI battery management system with per-module monitoring, Modbus
  integration to the UPS controls and BMS head-end, dry-contact alarm output
  to the FACP.
