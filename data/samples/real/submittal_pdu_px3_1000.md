# Vendor Submittal — Intelligent Rack PDU
_Project Helios DC-2 · Submittal SUB-HELIOS-PDU · Rev A_

Proposed unit: **Raritan PX3-1493V** (PX3-1000 "Monitored" series) intelligent
rack PDU, Zero-U vertical, 3-phase. All figures are Raritan's published PX3
series specifications.

## 1. Metering — PX3-1000 series

- **Metering scope.** Energy metering at the **inlet** (unit level): kWh, V, A,
  kW, kVA, PF, with per-line and per-breaker display on the color TFT.
  (Per-outlet metering is provided by the PX3-5000 outlet-metered series,
  which is not part of this proposal.)
- **Metering accuracy.** Billing-grade **+/- 1%** energy metering, certified to
  **ISO/IEC 62053-21**; EU 2014/32 MID accuracy compliant.

## 2. Outlet control

- **Outlet switching.** Not available on the PX3-1000 series — outlets are
  always-on (unswitched). (Outlet switching is a PX3-5000 series feature.)

## 3. Environment / mechanical

- **Operating temperature.** Rated for **60 C** maximum ambient operation.
- **Form factor.** Zero-U vertical, 3-phase 400 V wye input, 24 outlets
  (20x C13, 4x C19).
- **Environmental sensors.** USB sensor port for Raritan SmartSensor
  temperature/humidity probes.
- Network: Ethernet, serial, USB-A/USB-B management.
