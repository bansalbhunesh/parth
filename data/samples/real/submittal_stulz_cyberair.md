# Vendor Submittal — Precision Cooling Package
_Project Helios DC-2 · Submittal SUB-HELIOS-3 · Rev A_

Proposed: STULZ CyberAir 3 direct-expansion (DX) computer-room air
conditioners. All figures below are the manufacturer's published ratings.

## 1. Computer-Room Air Conditioners — STULZ CyberAir 3 DX

- **Fans.** All-EC plug fans, backward-inclined, direct-driven, mounted in the
  cabinet — variable speed for part-load efficiency.
- **Refrigerant.** **R410A** direct-expansion refrigerant.
- **Redundancy.** Proposed as **N+1** per data hall (one standby unit per room).
- **Net sensible cooling.** The selected CyberAir 3 model provides **180 kW**
  net sensible cooling at the design return-air condition (units are available
  across the CyberAir 3 range up to 730 kW).
- **Supply-air temperature.** Controlled to a **24 degrees C** supply setpoint.
