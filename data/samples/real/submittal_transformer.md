# Vendor Submittal — Dry-Type Distribution Transformer
_Project Helios DC-2 · Submittal SUB-HELIOS-8 · Rev A_

Proposed: cast-resin dry-type distribution transformers.

## 1. Dry-Type Transformer

- **Harmonic rating.** Standard design, **K-1** (not specially rated for harmonic
  load).
- **Insulation class.** **Class F (155 degrees C)**, cast-resin.
- **Climatic / fire class.** **F1** per IEC 60076-11.
- **Vector group.** **Dyn11**.
- **Impedance.** **6%** at rated load.
