# Vendor Submittal — Aspirating Smoke Detection
_Project Helios DC-2 · Submittal SUB-HELIOS-ASD · Rev A_

Proposed detector: **Xtralis VESDA VLC (LaserCOMPACT, VLC-800 class)**
aspirating smoke detector. All figures are Xtralis's published VLC
specifications.

## 1. Coverage / sensitivity — VESDA VLC

- **Coverage per detector.** Up to **800 m²** (8,000 sq ft) — the published
  maximum protected area for the LaserCOMPACT class.
- **Sensitivity.** Wide range **0.005 – 20 % obscuration/m**
  (0.0015 – 6 % obscuration/ft).
- **Listing.** EN 54-20 listed; UL/FM approvals per the VLC datasheet.

## 2. Pipe network / interface

- **Sampling network.** Engineered pipe network per the **ASPIRE** design
  tool, sampling the ceiling void and the supply-air path of the zone served.
- **Interfaces.** Relay outputs to the FACP; VESDAnet option for staged
  Alert / Action / Fire thresholds.
- **Power.** **24 V dc** from the monitored fire-power supply.
