# Vendor Submittal — UPS Battery System
_Project Helios DC-2 · Submittal SUB-HELIOS-7 · Rev A_

Proposed: valve-regulated lead-acid (VRLA) stationary batteries.

## 1. Battery System

- **Type.** VRLA, **general-purpose / standard commercial** class.
- **Design life.** **3 to 5 years** at 25 degrees C (EUROBAT standard-commercial).
- **Autonomy.** **10 minutes** at full rated load.
- **Monitoring.** String-level voltage monitoring; internal-resistance monitoring
  available as an option, not included.
