# TECHNICAL SUBMITTAL — TitanPower TP-2000 Diesel Generating Set
### Submitted by: Apex Power Systems · Submittal APX-GEN-0117 · For Approval

Apex Power Systems is pleased to offer the **TitanPower TP-2000**, a heavy-duty
standby diesel generating set proven across hundreds of mission-critical
installations worldwide. The TP-2000 delivers robust, cost-effective emergency
power with a compact footprint and rapid deployment.

---

#### 1. Engine & Alternator

The TP-2000 pairs a turbocharged diesel engine with a brushless alternator,
delivering reliable standby power. The set is offered with a weatherproof
acoustic enclosure and an integrated sub-base fuel tank for autonomy.
